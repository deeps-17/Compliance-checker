# DPDP Act 2023 Compliance Report

**Overall Compliance**: 55/100 - Partial Compliance

**Summary**: Zepto's privacy policy demonstrates a foundational framework for data protection but falls significantly short of the strict requirements introduced by India's DPDP Act 2023. While the policy successfully details Data Principal rights, appoints a Grievance Officer, and details cross-border data transfer practices, it suffers from critical gaps in the pillars of Children's Data, Consent, and Notice. Most notably, it relies on bundled/implied consent, fails to offer multi-lingual (Eighth Schedule) access, completely lacks verifiable parental consent mechanisms, does not explicitly prohibit tracking or targeted advertising directed at children, and fails to state its statutory obligation to notify the DPBI and users of data breaches.

---

### 🟡 Consent — Partial (Section 6)
**Assessment**:
The policy relies on bundled, implied, and unconditional consent mechanisms ('By visiting the Platform... You accept and agree...'). This directly violates the DPDP Act's mandate that consent must be free, specific, informed, unconditional, and given through a clear affirmative action. Additionally, there is no provision allowing users to access the consent request in languages listed in the Eighth Schedule of the Constitution.

**Evidence**:
*By visiting the Platform or setting up/creating an user account (Account) on the Platform, You accept and agree to be bound by the terms and conditions of this Privacy Notice and consent to Us collecting, storing, processing, transferring and sharing information...*

---

### 🟡 Notice — Partial (Section 5)
**Assessment**:
While the policy describes the categories of data collected and their purposes, it fails to explicitly name the 'Data Protection Board of India' (DPBI) and outline the precise manner in which a complaint can be filed with the Board. Furthermore, it fails to offer the mandatory option to access the notice in regional languages of the Eighth Schedule.

**Evidence**:
*Right to complain to supervisory authorities: If You wish to make a complaint... You have the right to complain to the appropriate supervisory authority of Your jurisdiction. However, We would suggest to first utilize Our grievance redressal mechanism...*

---

### 🟢 Data Principal Rights — Pass (Sections 11, 12, 13, and 14)
**Assessment**:
The policy comprehensively aligns with the Act by explicitly acknowledging and providing mechanisms for the exercising of the rights to access, correction, completion, updating, erasure, grievance redressal, and nomination.

**Evidence**:
*You can access and review Your Personal Information shared by You... You may review, correct, complete, upgrade, erase, update and change the information... Right to nominate another person: You have the right to nominate one or more individuals to act on Your behalf...*

---

### 🟡 Data Fiduciary Obligations — Partial (Section 8)
**Assessment**:
While Zepto details reasonable security safeguards and purpose-bound retention, the policy fails to mention the mandatory statutory obligation to notify the Data Protection Board of India and affected Data Principals in the event of a personal data breach. It also shifts the burden of ensuring complete and accurate data heavily onto the user, whereas the Act mandates the Fiduciary to ensure accuracy when making decisions.

**Evidence**:
*It is Your responsibility to promptly inform Us of any actual or suspected compromise of Your Personal Information. ... We can only keep Your Personal Information up-to-date and accurate to the extent You provide Us with the necessary information.*

---

### 🔴 Children's Data — Fail (Section 9)
**Assessment**:
The policy fails to comply with the DPDP Act's strict restrictions on children's data. It utilizes self-declaration/implied parental consent, which does not constitute 'verifiable consent' under Section 9(1). Crucially, the policy fails to prohibit tracking, behavioral monitoring, or targeted advertising directed at children, which is an absolute statutory bar.

**Evidence**:
*In case You are under the age of 18 years, You (i) accept and acknowledge that You are accessing the Platform through a parent or a legal guardian... and such person has accepted this Privacy Notice on Your behalf... We do not intentionally gather or request Personal Information from individuals under the age of 18.*

---

### 🟢 Grievance Officer — Pass (Section 13 and Section 8(9))
**Assessment**:
The policy successfully complies with the Act by publishing the name, designation, and direct email address of the Grievance Officer, ensuring that Data Principals have a readily available means of grievance redressal.

**Evidence**:
*You can contact the designated Grievance Officer for the purpose of this Privacy Notice, namely, Panduranga Acharya, Designation: General Counsel, at legal@zepto.com.*

---

### 🟢 Cross-border Data Transfer — Pass (Section 16)
**Assessment**:
The policy complies with transparency expectations under the Act by informing the user that personal data may be processed and stored outside India, specifying both third-party cloud locations (AWS) and external service provider jurisdictions.

**Evidence**:
*The Personal Information We process may be transferred to countries outside Your current location... the Platform stores Your data with the cloud platform of Amazon Web Services... which may store this data on their servers located outside of India.*
