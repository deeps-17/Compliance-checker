# DPDP Act 2023 Compliance Report

**Overall Compliance**: 45/100

**Summary**: The policy is primarily designed around the outdated Information Technology Rules 2011 and Aadhaar Act frameworks. It fails to meet critical modern compliance standards established by India's DPDP Act 2023, specifically regarding explicit affirmative consent, notice of the right to complain to the DPBI, verifiable parental consent mechanisms for children, protection of minors from tracking/targeted advertising, and the right to nominate. Significant revisions are required to align with the new statutory obligations.

---

### 🟡 Consent — Partial (Section 6)
**Assessment**:
The policy relies on implied consent through mere platform usage, which directly violates the DPDP Act's mandate for consent to be unambiguous and given via a clear affirmative action. While the policy references explicit consent for specific hardware-based permissions like SMS and camera, the baseline platform consent model is non-compliant. It also lacks multilingual options for consent requests.

**Evidence**:
*By visiting, downloading, using PhonePe Platform, and/or, providing your information... you expressly agree to be bound by this Privacy Policy... based on your explicit consent through your app permissions*

---

### 🟡 Notice — Partial (Section 5)
**Assessment**:
The notice lists the categories of data collected and purposes of processing. However, it fails to inform Data Principals of their right to make a complaint to the Data Protection Board of India (DPBI) and does not provide an option to access the notice in any of the languages specified in the Eighth Schedule to the Constitution.

**Evidence**:
*This Privacy Policy is published and shall be construed in accordance with the provisions of Indian laws... In case you have any questions, concerns, or complaints... you may write to PhonePe's Privacy Officer using this link https://support.phonepe.com*

---

### 🟡 Data Principal Rights — Partial (Sections 11, 12, 13, and 14)
**Assessment**:
The policy recognizes the rights to access, rectification, erasure, and grievance redressal. However, it completely fails to acknowledge or establish a mechanism for the right to nominate a representative in the event of death or incapacity under Section 14 of the DPDP Act.

**Evidence**:
*You can access and review your Personal Information shared by you... In case you wish to delete your account or Personal Information, please use the 'Help' section... nominee details [mentioned only as collected data, not as a dynamic right to nominate]*

---

### 🟡 Data Fiduciary Obligations — Partial (Section 8)
**Assessment**:
The policy outlines security safeguards and data deletion schedules. However, it improperly shifts the burden of identifying and reporting breaches onto the user, entirely omitting PhonePe's statutory obligation to proactively notify the DPBI and affected individuals of a personal data breach. Retention rules are also overly broad, citing 'other legitimate purposes' instead of strictly limiting retention to purpose fulfillment or legal compliance.

**Evidence**:
*It shall be your responsibility to intimate us in case of any actual or suspected compromise to your Personal Information... Once the Personal Information has reached its retention period, it shall be deleted... However, we may retain Personal Information related to you... for other legitimate purposes.*

---

### 🔴 Children's Data — Fail (Section 9)
**Assessment**:
The policy fails to establish a process for obtaining 'verifiable parental consent' as required by Section 9(1), shifting the responsibility to the user by requiring parental 'supervision' of minors. Additionally, it contains no provisions prohibiting processing that causes detrimental effects on children, nor does it prohibit tracking, behavioural monitoring, or targeted advertising directed at children.

**Evidence**:
*We do not knowingly solicit or collect Personal Information from children under the age of 18... If you are under the age of 18 years then you must use the Platform or services under the supervision of your parent, legal guardian, or any responsible adult.*

---

### 🟡 Grievance Officer — Partial (Section 13 and Section 8(9))
**Assessment**:
The policy references a 'Privacy Officer' and provides a generic support ticket web link, but it fails to publish the specific business contact details (such as a direct email address, phone number, or physical address) of a designated Grievance Officer or Data Protection Officer as required by the Act.

**Evidence**:
*you may write to PhonePe's Privacy Officer using this link https://support.phonepe.com*

---

### 🟢 Cross-border Data Transfer — Pass (Section 16)
**Assessment**:
The policy explicitly states that personal information is stored within India. It transparently notes that data may be shared with investigation agencies located outside India only for narrow legal or compliance purposes, which is compliant under the current framework of Section 16.

**Evidence**:
*To the extent applicable, we store Personal Information within India and retain it in accordance with applicable laws... with agencies appointed by PhonePe for investigation purposes located within or outside the Indian jurisdiction*
